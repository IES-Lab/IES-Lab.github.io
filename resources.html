Thank you for your interest in our work! We are actively seeking talented and motivated individuals to join our group. Please refer to the information below before reaching out.

  • For prospective graduate students, please follow our department's graduate admissions process and indicate your interest in joining our group in your statement of purpose. You are welcome to contact the PI, but we may not be able to respond individually before the formal application review process begins.
  • For current UCF students, please complete this form and notify the PI by email.
  • For prospective postdoctoral researchers, we currently have no open positions.
  • For visiting scholars and research interns, we welcome applications for long-term visits (six months or longer). Please complete this form.
Due to the high volume of inquiries we receive, we may not be able to respond to every message individually. The PI will reach out to selected candidates to arrange in-person or virtual interviews or discuss potential research opportunities as appropriate.
