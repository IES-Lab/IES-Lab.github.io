Welcome to the Intelligent Embodied Systems Laboratory (IES Lab) at the University of Central Florida! Our research goal is to develop general-purpose embodied autonomy that scales across diverse physical systems.

We pursue principled and scalable solutions for embodied intelligence through three complementary research directions:

  • Representations for effective skill transfer across diverse robot embodiments
  • Sustainable methods for large-scale robot data collection
  • Hardware design for dexterous robotic manipulation
